Online Education

The course of study accesssed entirely over the internet is known as online study

Advantages of online study:

  1. Flexibility
  2. Cost Effective
  3. Accessibility

Disadvantages of online study:

  • Reliance on technology
  • Not suitable for every topic
  • Difficulty in adapting

Explanation

Flexibility
We can take our class anywhere
Reliance on technology
