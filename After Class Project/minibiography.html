Mini Biography: Quaid-e-Azam

Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah was a barrister, statesman, and the founder of Pakistan. He is known as the "Founder of the Nation"

Early Life

He was born on December 25, 1876, in Karachi to a prosperous merchant family.

He received his early education at the Sindh Madrasah and later at the Mission School, Karachi.

Higher Education

After completing his matriculation examination at age 16, Jinnah enrolled at Lincoln's Innlaw school in 1893.

Political Career

Jinnah got the title of the best ambassador of Hindu-Muslim unity before joining the Muslim league.

Achievements

His landmark achievements span a distuinguished lagal and political career.


His most notable achievement is the creation of theworld's largest independent Muslim state in 1947 known as Pakistan.
